AssessWeeks 1 to 4
  • Interview department heads and map current processes
  • Score digital maturity: systems, data quality, skills
  • Build the process inventory (use the Prioritize tab)
PrioritizeWeeks 5 to 6
  • Score processes on value, feasibility, and risk
  • Choose 3 to 5 quick-win pilots
DesignWeeks 7 to 10
  • Fix the process before automating it
  • Define architecture, data flows, and access controls
Build and pilotWeeks 11 to 18
  • Build automations and run user acceptance testing
  • Measure the pilot against the baseline
Scale and governWeeks 19 to 26
  • Roll out to other departments
  • Set up a Center of Excellence for standards and the idea backlog
  • Train staff and manage change
